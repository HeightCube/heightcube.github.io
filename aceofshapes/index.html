Description

Neon shapes are falling. How long can you hold the line?

Ace of Shapes: Tower Defense is a fast, colourful tower defense made for phones. Shapes rain down from the top of the screen, your towers hold the bottom, and every wave hits harder than the last.

  • Build as you fight: the game never stops for the shop. Every kill pays: build towers, level them up and unlock their special skills while the next wave closes in.
  • Know your enemy: triangles dive, squares shrug off weak hits with their armour, circles hide behind regenerating shields, hexagons split in three. Every few waves a giant boss shape attacks.
  • Six towers, your strategy: rapid Pulse, explosive Burst, freezing Frost, chaining Arc, armour-piercing Lance and the supporting Beacon.
  • Stronger every run: earn Prisms, grow a passive tree, research new towers and specials, and climb five difficulty tiers.
  • Made for short sessions: portrait, one-handed, pause any time and resume where you left off. A daily run, a Weekly Anomaly with a new rule every week, ten bosses, achievements and leaderboards with Google Play Games.

HeightCube is a one-person studio based in Geneva, Switzerland, making apps for kids and free-to-play games. Ace of Shapes is its first game.
