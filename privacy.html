Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026
Controller: HeightCube, an independent studio based in Geneva, Switzerland. Contact: heightcube+website (at) gmail (dot) com

1. Summary

Our apps for kids do not show ads, do not require an internet connection, and do not collect, store or share personal data. Our games are free to play and may show ads or offer in-app purchases; section 3 explains what we will publish about them. This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers.

2. Our apps for kids

  • Our apps for kids work fully offline and do not request network access.
  • We do not collect personal information, device identifiers, location, or usage analytics.
  • We do not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking SDKs.
  • Any data created in an app (for example settings or progress) stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Some of them let a grown-up save a player’s progress to a file, for example to move it to another phone. The file goes only where you choose to put it.
  • Children: they are designed to be safe: no ads, no links out, no accounts, no data collection.
  • Distribution: all our apps are distributed through Google Play. Google may process data under its own privacy policy when you download or update an app. We do not receive that data in identifiable form.

3. Our games

Our first game is still in development and is not available yet. Games may show ads or offer in-app purchases, which usually rely on third-party services (for example an advertising network, or Google Play for payments) that process some data. Before a game is released, we will update this policy to say which services it uses, what data they process and what choices you have. Nothing in this section changes how our apps for kids work.

4. This website

  • Hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address in server logs. We do not have access to or use this data. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.
  • We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising or external fonts and scripts.

5. Contact by email

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We do not share it with third parties and delete it when no longer needed. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail), which processes messages under its own privacy policy.

6. Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the GDPR, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you. Since we collect almost none, this will usually only concern your email correspondence. Write to heightcube+website (at) gmail (dot) com.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
