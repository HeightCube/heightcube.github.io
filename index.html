For kids

FlagQuest 4 Kids

A geography quiz for kids: flags, shapes, capitals and maps.

Coming soon to Google Play

Explore the world, one flag at a time. Short rounds get harder as your child improves, and the app remembers which countries each player still needs to practise. After every answer, a fact card shows the flag, the shape, the country on the world map, its capital and its neighbours.

Ten ways to play, all 198 countries, several players on one phone, 46 badges to collect, questions read aloud for younger players. In English and French.

  • No ads

    Nothing is collected. Progress stays on the phone.
