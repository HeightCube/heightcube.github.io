What we do

Small apps, made with care

We design and build Android apps that are calm, honest and made to last. Each one does a few things well, respects your attention, and works wherever you are.

No dark patterns, no ads, no data quietly leaving your phone. Just thoughtful software we would happily hand to our own families.
